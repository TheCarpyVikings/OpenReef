What the software can—and cannot—prove

Verified source and simulated tests can check pin assignments, naming contracts, calculation and fault logic. A successful compile cannot prove your wiring, pump direction, actual dose, thermal behaviour, siphon protection or power cutoff.

The reservoir-low entities are permanently clear template stubs on this node. A full auto-bind count does not mean physical floats are fitted. Reported switch state and completed motor commands are not measured liquid flow.

Keep a physically limited test volume. For unattended operation, independent cutoff and physical level/leak protection remain additional safeguards to resolve.
