# End-of-beta close-out — template

The research on beta programs is emphatic about one omission: when the beta
ends, most teams simply go quiet. No thank-you, no summary, no sign the
testers' effort changed anything — which quietly burns the exact people most
likely to become advocates, references, and testers of the *next* thing.

This is the template for not doing that. It is sent as a portal
**Announcement** (Testers → Announcements → publish), so it lands in every
active tester's panel — plus personally by email to anyone who gave real time.

Fill the brackets from the portal before sending: the inbox stats tiles have
the totals, and the feedback list filtered to `actioned` is the "you changed
this" list.

---

## Announcement: title

> The beta did its job — thank you

## Announcement: body

> That's a wrap on this phase of the OpenReef beta, and it ends better than it
> started because of you.
>
> The numbers: [N] testers sent [N] reports. [N] were actioned — real fixes
> and features that exist because someone pressed the button instead of
> shrugging. The ones that mattered most: [pick 2–3 actioned items with real
> impact, quoted briefly — e.g. "the ATO double-run report that became the
> duty-cycle guard"].
>
> What happens next: [next phase — open beta / release / new cohort]. Your
> install keeps working exactly as it is; [what changes for them, if
> anything — e.g. "the feedback button stays" / "the beta feedback system
> retires at the end of the month, and the panel simply loses the button"].
>
> If you told me something that got marked "won't do" — thank you anyway. A
> clear no is a decision you helped make.
>
> The manual credits page lists every tester who wants to be on it. If you'd
> like your name (or tank name!) there, reply to this or say so in the
> feedback button. If you'd rather not, that's the default — nobody appears
> without asking.
>
> Go feed your fish. — Reece

---

## Checklist when the day comes

- [ ] Fill the numbers from the portal inbox stats and the `actioned` filter.
- [ ] Personal email (not just the announcement) to the top handful of
      contributors — name the specific thing of theirs that shipped.
- [ ] Decide the feedback system's fate and say it plainly in the message
      (retiring it = the removal checklist in `docs/beta-feedback.md`).
- [ ] Offer, don't assume, public credit.
- [ ] Export the feedback table first if the Supabase project is being wound
      down — it is the written history of why the product is shaped like this.
